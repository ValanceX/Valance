# Baseline, 2026-10-24, before WP8

The sixth baseline, and the first taken after the features of WP3 to WP7 (units, forms, layers, the primitive specification, the style contract, replay). The earlier ones are not edited. It is what the ceilings of `packages/cli/src/budgets.json` are set from, and what the [size analysis](../evidence/measurement/size-2026-10-24.md) explains.

## How it was measured

`node scripts/measure-footprint.mjs --tree --runs 3 --updates 200 --json before.json` on the tree at commit `7535a3e` of `claude/optimistic-gates-9xmvhs` (WP8 not started; the workspace's `cli`, `common`, `valance`, `router` and `style` packed and installed beside the published `mesh-runtime` 0.15.0, `nexus` 0.14.0 and `port-web` 0.12.0), Node 22.22.0, Chromium 141.0.7390.37 through `playwright-core` 1.56.1, a 4-vCPU container, medians of 3 fresh browsers, with and without 4x CPU throttling, localhost. The harness was changed to measure this (it packs `style`, defaults to the runtime Valance depends on, and records an installability refusal instead of crashing on it). One machine: the millisecond rows are for it only.

## Updates, wire, installability

`update` of 10,000 items, one changed: 100.1 ms (46.7 ms from changes). Wire for one changed row of 1,000: 185,924 B as a whole tree, **202 B as patches**. The newest published set does **not** install together: `npm` refuses `@valancex/mesh-runtime@0.16.0` beside `@valancex/nexus@0.14.0` and `@valancex/port-web@0.12.0`, whose peer ranges stop at 0.15 (fixed by NEXUS 0.15 and PORT 0.13, WP8).

## Against the ceilings of the 2026-10-17 baseline

Four were crossed before WP8 changed anything: the WebAssembly runtime (183,629 against 179,400), the counter's JavaScript (93,722 against 92,400), its transfer (279,594 against 274,100) and `docs-app`'s JavaScript (98,520 against 97,400). The size analysis is why, and the new ceilings are these figures plus 5 percent.
